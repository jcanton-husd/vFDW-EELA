Unit 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is no longer a concept of the far future. Today, it forms the backbone of web search engine architectures, safety features in vehicles, predictive texting, and dynamic data structures. Let's explore the core definitions, structures, and concepts our students cover in class.

01

What is Artificial Intelligence?

The science of training computer systems to emulate cognitive tasks typically performed by humans, such as visual perception, pattern recognition, decision-making, and natural language translation.

02

Machine Learning (ML) vs. Deep Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of AI centered around structural algorithms that analyze and learn from statistical data models. Deep Learning scales this using multi-layered Artificial Neural Networks modelled loosely after biological neural networks.

03

The Power of Generative Models

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs) utilize transformer architectures to process vast arrays of tokenized text to compute structural relationships, answering complex prompts with contextual clarity.
